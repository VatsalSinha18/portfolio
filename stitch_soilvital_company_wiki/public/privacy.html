Privacy Policy

How SoilVital handles contact details, farm inquiries, and service information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

We collect contact details, farm location or district, service requests, and messages you submit through SoilVital forms. We may also collect basic technical data such as browser type, IP address, and request timestamps to protect the service and improve reliability.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, schedule soil testing, provide agronomy consultation, prepare quotations, and improve SoilVital services. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and Storage

Information may be shared with SoilVital team members, field collection partners, lab partners, or service providers only when needed to fulfill your request. Lead records are stored securely and retained only as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal, or customer support purposes.

Your Choices

You can request correction or deletion of your submitted information by contacting us. If you receive marketing communication from SoilVital, you can opt out at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@soilvital.com.
