Careers

Work with SoilVital to build practical soil intelligence for farmers, agronomists, and local agriculture partners.

Why Join SoilVital

SoilVital brings agronomy, field operations, lab science, and software together to help farmers make better soil decisions. We look for practical builders who care about measurable farm outcomes.

Open Areas

  • Field agronomy and farmer success
  • Soil sample collection and operations
  • Lab coordination and quality control
  • IoT sensor deployment and support
  • Sales, partnerships, and local market growth

How to Apply

Send your resume, preferred role, and city of operation to careers@soilvital.com. Include a short note about the farm, soil, or technology problem you would be excited to solve.

Internships and Field Projects

Students and early-career professionals can apply for field research, farmer onboarding, and data collection projects. We value curiosity, reliability, and comfort working directly with farmers.
